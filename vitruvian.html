Vitruvian Trainer+

Smart strength training

A smart strength-training platform that uses adaptive resistance technology to deliver personalized, data-driven workouts at home. Innovative hardware paired with an intelligent app tracks performance, adjusts weight in real time, and optimizes every rep for safer, more effective training.

My role: built iOS features in Swift, SwiftUI and UIKit and, with the Android team, implemented the Bluetooth Low Energy communication layer in Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP, packaged as an SPM module) to talk to Vitruvian's connected hardware. Also delivered new UI flows, supported incident management for app stability, and wrote unit tests feeding the CI/CD pipeline.
